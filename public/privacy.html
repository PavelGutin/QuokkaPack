QuokkaPack Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What QuokkaPack stores

QuokkaPack stores your packing catalog, trips, and checklists in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on your device. Nothing is sent to any server operated by QuokkaPack or its developer. The app's files, including its fonts and icons, are served by GitHub Pages, which may log basic request information such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Google Drive sync (optional)

QuokkaPack is adding an optional Google Drive sync. If you choose to connect Google Drive, QuokkaPack requests only the drive.appdata permission. This lets the app save and read its own data file in a hidden application data folder in your Google Drive. That file contains the same catalog, trips, and checklists the app stores in your browser. With this permission, QuokkaPack cannot see, read, or change any of your other Drive files.

This data goes directly between your browser and Google. The developer never receives or has access to it.

What we don't do

  • No QuokkaPack accounts, analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • Google user data is used only to provide the sync feature, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

To remove local data, use Backup → Erase everything in the app, or clear your browser's site data for this site.

To revoke QuokkaPack's access to Google Drive, go to myaccount.google.com/connections, select QuokkaPack, and remove its access. To delete the hidden data file, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find QuokkaPack, and choose Options → Delete hidden app data.
